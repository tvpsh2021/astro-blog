淺淺草先生
Design guide, version 2

A personal notebook kept since 2007

The blog mixes diary entries and technical notes, with long quiet stretches between busy years. The design shows that history plainly: one activity grid on the homepage, posts grouped by year, and a reading column sized for Traditional Chinese. Everything else stays quiet. The live source of truth is src/styles/global.css; update this page when it changes.

Principles

One bold elementThe homepage activity grid is the only expressive element. Do not add a second hero, gradients, or decorative motion.
Structure carries informationRules separate years and entries. No sequence numbers, no all-caps eyebrow labels, no arrows appended to links. A title is the link to its post.
Chinese firstSizes, spacing, and line length are tuned for Traditional Chinese. Headings use zero letter spacing; negative tracking squeezes CJK glyphs together.
Reader controlsAll sizes are in rem so browser font settings apply. Readers can choose automatic, light, or dark appearance.

Colors and themes

Paper is a slightly green-grey off-white, not a warm cream. Green is the only brand color. Each swatch shows light on the left and dark on the right. Colors are defined once with light-dark(); the page follows the OS setting unless the reader picks a theme.

Typography

One family: Noto Sans TC from Google Fonts at 400, 500, and 700, with PingFang TC and Microsoft JhengHei as local fallbacks. It is free, open source, and renders reliably across platforms. Latin text uses Noto Sans TC's own glyphs so mixed text stays consistent. Code uses the system monospace stack.

Article text also uses line-break: strict so closing punctuation never starts a line, text-autospace: normal for a thin gap between CJK and Latin where supported, and overflow-wrap: break-word so English words and URLs only break when they must. Body text is left aligned, not justified.

Components

Previews use placeholder content. The rendered site uses WordPress data through the Astro components listed under Maintenance.

Activity grid (ActivityGrid.astro): one column per year, one cell per month, every year from the first post to the latest, including empty ones. Each year with posts is a link to the archive page holding that year; hovering a cell shows its month and count. The grid sits in a <details> labelled 寫作紀錄, open by default. Collapsing it stores activity=closed in localStorage, and an inline script right after the element keeps it closed on later visits without a flash. No title, caption, or legend: the form is familiar enough to read on its own.
